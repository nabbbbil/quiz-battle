# Quiz Battle design direction

Answers from the project owner (29 Sep 2026), transcribed as given.

- **Energy:** friendly, balanced. Clearly a game, but calmer than a game show: solid colors, rounded shapes, less shouting. Easy to read in a noisy room.
- **Main color:** operator red, the red of the + − × ÷ tiles in the owner's Godot Math Quiz Game, on white/off-white. It ties Quiz Battle to that game.
- **Theme:** light only.
- **UI rules:** antislop, applied during the build.

Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1
